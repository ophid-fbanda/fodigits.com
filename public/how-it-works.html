The arrangement

You keep the keys.

We write the software. You run it. That is the whole idea — the same whether it is an asset book or a video room.

01

Pick from the catalog

Each product has editions: Home for a small place, Pro for a working office, White-label when the product should wear your name.

02

Download a package

You get a starter kit — compose file, license note, environment example. The browser treats it as a real download because it is one.

03

Bring it up locally

On a VPS, a machine in the back office, or a clinic cupboard. HTTPS on your domain. Backups on your disk.

What we do not do

We do not host your patients, your stock, or your meetings. We do not keep a copy of the work you do inside the product.

If you later want us to install it for you, that is a separate conversation — not the default.

What a kit contains

README, license, docker-compose, and an env example. Runtime images are meant to pull on first launch once a license is in place.

Requirements are listed on every product page so you can size the box before you start.
