The studio

We make software people take home.

FoDigits is a small studio. We build tools that a school, a clinic, or a workshop can download and run without sending their work to a rented cloud they do not understand.

The first products came out of jobs we had already done — an asset register that had to live on a local machine, a video room a doctor could open without a third-party account. The catalog is how those tools become something you can pick up.

We are not a hosting company. If you need a server, you already know how to rent one. If you need the software to wear your name, that is what white-label is for.
